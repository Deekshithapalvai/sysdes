Problem 2: Employee Salary and Bonus Management System

A company has different employee categories:
1. Permanent Employee – 20% bonus
2. Contract Employee – 10% bonus
3. Intern – 5% bonus
Business requirements change:
- Senior Developer – 25% bonus
- Manager – 30% bonus
- Performance-based bonus – depends on performance score.
Your requirements:
1. Create an abstract class BonusStrategy.
2. Create individual bonus implementations.
3. Create an Employee class.
4. Create a BonusCalculator that accepts a bonus strategy.
5. Adding a new bonus strategy must not require modifying BonusCalculator.
6. Validate salary and bonus inputs.
